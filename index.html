Supported Plate Styles & Sizes

The bracket is designed around a broad range of plate footprints. Final compatibility depends on the plate's physical dimensions, hole pattern, and the selected face-bracket configuration.

Plate footprint

Universal Footprint Coverage

The mounting system is designed to accommodate plate footprints from approximately 190mm × 83mm up to 520mm × 166mm, subject to the appropriate face-bracket configuration.

Australian mounting

Standard 210mm Mounting Pitch

Compatible configurations can use the stated 210mm horizontal mounting pitch at the inner mounting points. Confirm the hole spacing and physical dimensions of your specific plate before ordering.

Full-size Euro

520mm Euro Plates

Full-size Euro plates may be supplied without mounting holes depending on the issuing authority and plate type. Direct mounting therefore depends on the plate being drilled to the required mounting spacing or used with a suitable adapter. Note: Due to the 520mm width, the full 3rd position "tuck" is restricted to maintain bumper clearance.

Specialty plates

Un-drilled, Enamel & Heritage Plates

Plates without suitable mounting holes will not necessarily bolt directly to the face bracket. An appropriate frame, adapter, or other mounting method may be required.

International

JDM / USA Configurations

JDM and USA-style plate configurations can be accommodated where the plate and selected face bracket provide a compatible mounting pattern.

Engineering Note

The bracket was developed to address common problems with universal plate mounts, particularly unwanted bumper drilling and movement or sag. The design is CAD-developed around the factory NSX mounting locations with the aim of providing a clean OEM+ installation without permanently altering the original bodywork.

Frequently Asked Questions

Click to Expand Frequently Asked Questions
What tools do I need for installation?
Installation requires 3mm and 4mm Allen (hex) keys. Allen keys are not included.
Does it fit NA1 and NA2?
Yes. The design is intended around both factory NA1 and NA2 front bumper configurations.
Do I need to drill my bumper?
No. The bracket is designed to use the existing factory mounting locations.
Can I return the car to stock?
The installation is intended to be reversible provided the original mounting points have not otherwise been modified.
What plate sizes can I run?
The stated supported footprint range is approximately 190mm × 83mm to 520mm × 166mm, subject to the selected face-bracket configuration and the plate's mounting pattern. See the Plate Fitment section above.
How does the plate attach?
Attachment depends on the selected face-bracket configuration and the mounting holes available on the plate. Plates without compatible holes may require drilling or an additional adapter/frame.
Does it work with aftermarket bumpers?
Fitment depends on whether the aftermarket bumper retains the relevant factory mounting points and spacing. Contact us before ordering if your bumper differs from factory configuration.
Can I use it on the street?
The owner is responsible for verifying local road transport requirements before using the product on public roads, including requirements relating to plate visibility, angle, height, lighting and obstruction.
What material is it made from?
Heavy-duty 1.6mm mild steel with a durable matte black coating.
Does it include hardware?
Yes. The bracket assembly, mounting hardware and setup guide are included. 3mm and 4mm Allen keys are required separately.
Will it work with a front lip?
Potentially. Fitment depends on whether the lip and bumper retain the relevant factory mounting locations and provide sufficient clearance.
